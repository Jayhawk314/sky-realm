## What's in Sky Realms

Sky Realms is one add-on file, **SkyRealms.mcaddon**, that turns your world into a sky world. You grow floating islands and fly between them on five different rides. It works on Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.110 or newer and doesn't need any Experiments turned on.

| Ride or item | What it is | Seats | Runs on | Special power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sky Fighter | Fast jet | 2 | Fuel | Fires twin lasers with the Sky Blaster |
| Hoverboard | Small, zippy board | 1 | Fuel | Quickest way to hop between islands |
| Cargo Blimp | Slow airship | 2 | Fuel (uses the least) | Carries a 27-slot cargo hold |
| Fire Dragon | Creature you hatch and raise | 2 when grown | Food | Breathes fireballs with the Sky Blaster |
| Sky Whale | Huge, gentle flying whale | 4 | Nothing | Drifts around by itself when nobody rides it |
| Peter | Your own giant star dragon, about 13 blocks long | 3 when grown | Food | Fastest ride. Shoots starfire with the Sky Blaster. |
| Race Rings + Race Flag | Glowing checkpoints and a race timer | none | none | Keeps a best-lap board for your course |
| Island Seed | Grows a floating island under you | none | none | Grass, dirt, stone, ores, flowers and a tree |

## World map

Build the whole world between **Y 160 and Y 220**, above the mountains. Spawn Isle sits in the middle and everything else is a short flight away. In world settings, turn on **Show Coordinates** so you can find each spot. East is +X and south is +Z.

&#91;image: Sky Realms world map: islands, routes and the 8-ring race circuit around Spawn Isle\]

| Zone | Where, in blocks from spawn | What goes there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn Isle & Hangar | Center | Kit storage, crafting, Race Flag start and finish line |
| Fuel Depot | 140 east, 110 south | Chests of coal, charcoal and blaze powder |
| Base: Brother 1 | 230 west | Your own home island |
| Base: Brother 2 | 230 east | Your own home island |
| Dragon Roost | 200 north | Rocky peak where the dragon eggs hatch. Build it highest. |
| Whale Drift | 170 west, 150 south | Open sky to release whales. They roam about 48 blocks from where they start. |
| Farm Isle | 210 east, 170 south | Cows and pigs for dragon food, sheep for wool, wheat |
| Fighter Range | 210 west, 170 north | Targets made of hay bales or wool for blaster practice |
| Race Circuit | Loop about 120 blocks around spawn | 8 rings, starting and finishing at Spawn Isle |

One Island Seed grows an island 12 to 22 blocks across. Plant several side by side to make big islands like the ones on the map.

## Install and set up the world

Whoever hosts the world installs the add-on. Your brother doesn't install anything. The packs download to him automatically when he joins.

1. On the hosting phone, tablet or Windows PC, open **SkyRealms.mcaddon** and choose Minecraft. Wait for "Successfully imported".
2. Go to **Play > Create New > Create New World**. Turn on **Show Coordinates**. Pick Creative to test everything fast, or Survival to craft it all.
3. Open **Behavior Packs > My Packs**, choose **Sky Realms (Behavior)** and press **Activate**. The resource pack turns on with it. If it doesn't, activate **Sky Realms (Resources)** under Resource Packs.
4. In the **Multiplayer** tab, keep Multiplayer Game on. Turn on Visible to LAN Players if you're on the same Wi-Fi.
5. Press **Create**, then invite your brother from the pause menu (**Invite to Game**). He can also join from the Friends tab. When he's asked to download the packs, he says yes.

To update later, open a newer SkyRealms.mcaddon the same way. It replaces the old version.

Xbox, PlayStation and Switch can't import add-on files. The host needs a phone, tablet or PC, and your brother can usually join from any device.

## How to get everything

In Creative, every item is in the creative inventory, and you can search for its name. You'll also find spawn eggs for the Fire Dragon and the Sky Whale. In Survival, you craft them at a crafting table.

| Item | Recipe | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sky Fighter Kit | Top row: feather in the middle. Middle row: iron ingot, redstone block, iron ingot. Bottom row: iron ingots in the two corners. | 1 |
| Cargo Blimp Kit | Top row: 3 white wool. Middle row: white wool, chest, white wool. Bottom row: 3 planks. | 1 |
| Hoverboard | Top row: 3 planks. Second row: feather, redstone, feather. | 1 |
| Sky Blaster | Top row: iron ingot, redstone, glowstone dust. Second row: stick in the middle. | 1 |
| Dragon Egg | Egg in the center, coal on the 4 sides, gold ingots in the 4 corners. | 1 |
| Whale Song | Note block + feather + raw cod, in any spots | 1 |
| Race Ring | Glowstone block + feather, in any spots | 2 |
| Race Flag | Stick + white wool + black wool, in any spots | 1 |
| Island Seed | Dirt + wheat seeds + feather, in any spots | 2 |
| Peter's Egg | The real Dragon Egg from beating the Ender Dragon in the center, eyes of ender on the 4 sides, end stone in the 4 corners. | 1 |

Fuel: coal or charcoal adds 25%, and blaze powder fills the tank to 100%.

## How each thing works

### Flying (every ride)

- Tap or right-click a ride to climb on. Move forward to fly where you're looking, and look down to dive.
- **Hold jump to climb.** Sneak to get off.
- If you jump off in midair, you get Slow Falling for 8 seconds, so you won't take fall damage.
- When nobody is flying them, vehicles rest on the ground.

### Fuel (Fighter, Hoverboard, Blimp)

- A new vehicle starts at 60%, and a fuel bar shows on screen while you ride.
- On a full tank, the Fighter flies about 3.5 minutes, the Hoverboard about 5 and the Blimp about 8.
- To refuel, hold coal, charcoal or blaze powder and tap the vehicle. On phones, a **Refuel** button appears.
- If you run out of fuel, the vehicle glides slowly down to the ground.

### Sky Blaster

- Hold the Blaster and use it while you ride the **Sky Fighter** to fire twin lasers, or while you ride a **grown Fire Dragon** to breathe a fireball.
- Fireballs set mobs on fire but never burn your builds. Lasers and fireballs can hit players too, so agree on the rules first.

### Packing up a vehicle

- Sneak and hit a vehicle with nobody on it, and it turns back into its kit item.
- When you pack up the blimp, everything in its cargo hold drops on the ground. A vehicle that gets destroyed also drops its kit.

### Cargo hold

- The crate at the back of the blimp is a 27-slot chest that flies with it. Tap it to open it.
- If the crate gets broken, its items drop, and the blimp gets a new empty crate about 10 seconds later.

### Fire Dragon

1. Place a Dragon Egg on the ground. It wobbles, then hatches after 2 minutes.
2. The baby picks the nearest player within 24 blocks as its owner and follows them around.
3. Feed it cooked meat or cooked fish. Each meal adds 10% growth, so 10 meals make it fully grown. If you don't feed it, it still grows up on its own in 20 minutes.
4. Once it's grown, tap it to ride. It has 2 seats and needs no fuel. Cooked meat also heals it.

Dragons from a spawn egg are wild adults. To tame one, feed it raw cod or salmon. Each fish has about a 1 in 3 chance to work.

### Sky Whale

- Use a Whale Song on a block, and a whale appears. It drifts around within about 48 blocks of where it started.
- It has 4 seats on its wooden deck and flies slowly and steadily. Raw fish heals it.

### Peter

Peter is the hardest ride to get, because you unlock him by beating the Ender Dragon.

&#91;image: Peter, the giant star dragon, and Peter's Egg, drawn from the add-on's own model and textures\]

1. Beat the Ender Dragon in the End and collect its Dragon Egg. To pick the egg up, dig out the block 2 below it and put a torch there. Then break the block right under the egg, and it falls onto the torch and drops as an item. In Bedrock the egg appears the first and second time you beat the dragon, so you can each get one.
2. Craft Peter's Egg and place it on the ground. It hatches after 3 minutes, and Peter picks the nearest player as his owner.
3. Feed him cooked meat. He needs 20 meals to grow up, or he grows up on his own in 40 minutes.
4. Grown Peter has 3 seats and is the fastest flyer in Sky Realms. He roars when he takes off, and the Sky Blaster shoots starfire (9 damage) while you ride him.

In Creative there's also a Peter spawn egg. A Peter from a spawn egg is wild: tame him with raw cod or salmon, which works about 1 time in 5.

### Sky Races

1. Use a **Race Ring** to build a glowing ring 7 blocks in front of you, facing the way you're looking. The rings are numbered in the order you place them, up to 40.
2. To remove the last ring you placed, sneak and use a Race Ring.
3. To start a race, use the **Race Flag**. After the 3-2-1-GO, fly through the rings in order while the timer runs.
4. When you finish, your time goes in the chat, along with personal bests and course records. Sneak and use the flag to see the top 10. Changing the course starts a new board.

### Island Seeds

- Fly up until you have at least 8 blocks of air below you, then use the seed. An island grows just under your feet, and you float down onto it.
- Each island is a random size, 12 to 22 blocks across, with ores, flowers and a tree. It only fills empty air, so it never breaks anything you've built.

## First play session

Tick off each step as you go. Steps 1 to 5 build the world, and steps 6 to 8 are the fun part.

- [ ] Host creates the world (see Install and set up) and the brother joins
- [ ] Fly or build up to about Y 180 above spawn and plant 3 to 4 Island Seeds to make Spawn Isle
- [ ] Build the Hangar and the Fuel Depot, and stock it with coal
- [ ] Each brother flies 230 blocks out, one west and one east, and grows a home island
- [ ] Grow the Dragon Roost 200 blocks north, the tallest island of all
- [ ] Each brother places a Dragon Egg at the Roost, then feeds his baby 10 cooked meals until it's grown
- [ ] Release a Sky Whale at Whale Drift and take a 4-seat ride
- [ ] Place 8 Race Rings in a loop around Spawn Isle and race for the course record
- [ ] Do a blimp cargo run between the two bases

* [ ] Boss quest: beat the Ender Dragon together, bring back its egg and hatch Peter

## What to test first, and what's next

This is version 1.1.0, which adds Peter. The files pass the Bedrock format checks, and the script was checked against Minecraft's script API 2.0.0. It hasn't been played inside Minecraft yet, so your first flight is the real test. Report back on these and they're quick to fix:

- [ ] Speed: is each ride too fast or too slow? Speeds are set relative to the Happy Ghast.
- [ ] Seats: do riders sit at the right height, or do they poke through the model?
- [ ] Cargo hold: does tapping the blimp's crate open it?
- [ ] Sky Blaster: does it fire when you use it on a phone while riding?

The models are simple, blocky first versions. You can swap any of them for your own Blockbench or Blender (Mcblend) model by keeping the same geometry name, for example geometry.sky\_dragon.

Ideas for version 2: wild dragon eggs that appear on mountain tops, sky pirate blimps to battle, a Magic Carpet, a boost button, and ring colors for different race courses.
