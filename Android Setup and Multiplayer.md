# Sky Realms on an Android Phone: Setup and Playing Together

These steps cover hosting Sky Realms on an Android phone and getting your brother into the world.

**Before you start:** update Minecraft from the Play Store on **both** devices. Sky Realms needs version 1.21.110 or newer, and both players must be on the same version to play together.

## Part 1: Put the add-on on the phone (host only)

1. **Get the file onto the phone.** It's at `SkyRealms.mcaddon` in this folder. Any of these work:
   - **Google Drive:** upload it on the PC. On the phone, open Drive, tap ⋮ on the file, then tap **Download**.
   - **Email:** send it to yourself and save the attachment on the phone.
   - **USB cable:** plug in the phone, pick "File transfer" and copy it into the phone's **Download** folder.
2. **Open it from the Files app,** not from inside Drive or Gmail. Open **Files** (Files by Google or "My Files"), go to **Downloads** and tap **SkyRealms.mcaddon**. If asked which app to use, pick **Minecraft**.
3. Minecraft opens and shows **"Import started"**, then **"Successfully imported"**. Wait for both messages. You'll see two messages, one for each pack.

If tapping the file doesn't offer Minecraft, long-press the file, choose **Open with** and pick Minecraft. If the name ends in `.zip` instead, rename it back to `.mcaddon`.

## Part 2: Make the world (host only)

1. In Minecraft, go to **Play → Create New → Create New World**.
2. **General:** pick **Creative** to try everything fast, or **Survival** to craft it all. Turn on **Show Coordinates**, which you'll need to find the islands.
3. **Behavior Packs:** under **Available**, tap **Sky Realms (Behavior)**, then **Activate**. If a box says the resource pack is needed too, say yes.
4. **Multiplayer:** turn on **Multiplayer Game** and **Visible to LAN Players**.
5. Tap **Create**.

To play an existing Sky Realms world, just open it. A newer SkyRealms.mcaddon replaces the old version, and the world uses it automatically.

## Part 3: Your brother joins

He doesn't install anything. The packs download to his device when he joins.

### Same Wi-Fi (easiest)

1. Host: have the world open and running.
2. Brother: go to **Play → Friends tab**. Your world appears under **LAN Games**. He taps it.
3. When asked to download the world's packs, he taps **Download & Join** (or Yes).

### Different places

1. Both of you sign in with a Microsoft (Xbox) account. The button is on the main menu.
2. Add each other as friends: **Play → Friends → Add Friend**, then search the other person's gamertag.
3. Host: with the world open, pause and tap **Invite to Game**, then pick your brother. Or he can open the **Friends** tab and join your world from **Joinable Friends Worlds**.

Your brother can join from another phone, a tablet, a PC, Xbox, PlayStation or Switch.

## If he can't join

- **Different Minecraft versions:** update both devices.
- **Child accounts:** if either of you is under 18, a parent may need to allow multiplayer. They can do that at account.xbox.com under Privacy & online safety ("You can join multiplayer games" set to Allow), or in the Xbox Family Settings app.
- **LAN game doesn't show up:** check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network, not one on mobile data or a guest network.
- **World not visible to friends:** check that Multiplayer Game is on in the world settings.

## Tips for hosting on a phone

- **The host must be playing.** Your brother can only be in the world while you have it open.
- Keep the phone **plugged in**, because hosting uses a lot of battery.
- If it lags, go to **Settings → Video** and lower **Render Distance** (and **Simulation Distance** in the world settings).
- The first trip to the Cloud Realm takes a few seconds to build, and it may lag for a moment. That's normal.

## Touch controls in Sky Realms

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Get on a ride | Tap it |
| Climb | Hold the jump button |
| Get off | Tap the sneak button (the round button in the middle of the arrow pad) |
| Refuel | Hold coal or blaze powder and tap the vehicle, or press the **Refuel** button |
| Use the Blaster, seeds, Race Rings or Portal Kit | Hold the item and tap the screen. If aiming is hard, turn on **Settings → Touch → Split controls** for a crosshair. |
| Pack up a ride | Turn sneak on, then hit the empty ride |

For recipes and how every ride and item works, see **Sky Realms Guide.md**.
