# Sky Realms Guide (version 1.2.0)

Sky Realms is one file, **SkyRealms.mcaddon**, for Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.110 or newer. No Experiments needed.

New in 1.2.0: bouncy **Cloud Blocks**, **Cloud Seeds**, and the **Cloud Portal** to the secret **Cloud Realm**, where a castle is hidden inside a giant cloud.

## Install

Hosting on an Android phone? Follow **Android Setup and Multiplayer.md** for step-by-step phone and joining instructions.

Only the host installs it. Your brother gets the packs automatically when he joins.

1. On the hosting phone, tablet or Windows PC, open **SkyRealms.mcaddon** and pick Minecraft. Wait for "Successfully imported".
2. **New world:** Create New World, turn on **Show Coordinates**, then under **Behavior Packs > My Packs** activate **Sky Realms (Behavior)**. The resource pack turns on with it.
   **Existing Sky Realms world:** nothing to do. Version 1.2.0 replaces 1.1.0 and the world uses it the next time you open it.
3. Keep Multiplayer on, start the world, and invite your brother. He says yes to downloading the packs.

Xbox, PlayStation and Switch can't import add-ons, so the host needs a phone, tablet or PC.

## How everything works

**Flying.** Tap a ride to get on. Look where you want to go, hold jump to climb, sneak to get off. Jumping off in midair gives you Slow Falling.

**Fuel (Fighter, Hoverboard, Blimp).** New vehicles start at 60%. Coal or charcoal adds 25%, and blaze powder fills the tank. Hold the fuel and tap the vehicle (phones get a **Refuel** button). A fuel bar shows while you ride. Out of fuel means you glide slowly down.

**Sky Blaster.** Use it while riding the Sky Fighter (twin lasers), a grown Fire Dragon (fireballs) or a grown Peter (starfire). Fireballs never burn builds.

**Packing up.** Sneak and hit an empty vehicle to turn it back into its kit. The blimp's cargo spills on the ground.

**Cargo Blimp.** 2 seats, slow, uses the least fuel. The crate on the back is a 27-slot chest that flies with it.

**Fire Dragon.** Place the Dragon Egg. It hatches in 2 minutes and bonds with the nearest player. Feed it 10 cooked meals to grow it up. Then it has 2 seats and breathes fireballs.

**Sky Whale.** Use a Whale Song on the ground. The whale has 4 seats on its deck and drifts within about 48 blocks of where it started.

**Peter.** Beat the Ender Dragon, bring back its egg and craft Peter's Egg. It hatches in 3 minutes. 20 cooked meals grow him up: 3 seats, the fastest ride, roars on take-off and shoots starfire.

**Race Rings and Race Flag.** Each Race Ring builds a glowing ring 7 blocks ahead of you, up to 40. Sneak and use a ring to remove the last one. Use the Race Flag for a 3-2-1-GO race through the rings in order. Sneak and use the flag to see the top-10 board.

**Island Seed.** With 8 or more blocks of air under you, it grows a floating island 12 to 22 blocks wide right under you. It only fills empty air.

**Cloud Blocks.** Place them like any block. Jump onto a cloud from high up and you bounce back up with no fall damage. Hold jump as you land to bounce higher, or sneak to stop bouncing. Standing on clouds also makes you jump higher.

**Cloud Seed.** Works like an Island Seed (8 blocks of air below you), but grows a puffy cloud island 12 to 21 blocks wide with bumps on top to bounce on.

**Cloud Portal.** Stand on the ground with open space in front of you and use the Cloud Portal Kit. A cloud frame with a glowing middle appears 3 blocks ahead. It needs a space 4 wide and 5 tall. Walk in, or fly in on a ride, and you go up to the Cloud Realm.

**The Cloud Realm.** The first trip takes a few seconds while the realm forms around you (you float gently until the landing pad appears). It's a huge cloud island with a giant cloud dome in the middle. Walk through the opening in the dome to find the **Cloud Castle**, with a treasure chest at the back. The chest fills only once, so share it! To go home, walk into the portal behind the landing pad and you return to the portal you came from. If you fall off the Cloud Realm without a ride or wings, the clouds catch you and put you back on the landing pad.
